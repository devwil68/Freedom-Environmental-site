Before you book

What can go in a dumpster?

We accept approved nonhazardous household and construction debris. Liberty will help confirm the right option for your project.

Generally accepted

Furniture, household debris, wood, drywall, flooring, fixtures, and nonhazardous renovation debris.

Owner approval required

Concrete, dirt, brick, tile, shingles, appliances, tires, mattresses, and unusually heavy materials.

We cannot accept

Hazardous materials, liquids, paint, chemicals, batteries, propane cylinders, medical waste, asbestos-containing material, or unknown drums.
